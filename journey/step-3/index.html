Join community

Missed Open Day? Step into the UTAS ecosystem. Join ICT clubs, build real projects, and find your professional circle.

Tech & Industry Clubs

UTAS Motorsport

HANDS-ONTECH

A student-driven Formula SAE team that designs, funds, builds and races its own open-wheel race car every year. Real project experience with industry sponsors — and members come from engineering, ICT, business and media.

Open in Google Maps ↗
When:
Year-round; recruitment at the start of each semester (follow UTAS Motorsport on Facebook)
Where:
School of Engineering, Sandy Bay campus
Who can join:
All students — curious beginners welcome
Official link
utas.edu.au — Motorsport team

SecTalks Hobart (Cybersecurity)

TECHNETWORKING

Connect with security enthusiasts. Free monthly talks, Capture The Flag (CTF) challenge nights and CTF101 workshops for beginners — UTAS and TasTAFE students are welcome, and there is pizza.

Open in Google Maps ↗
When:
Second Tuesday of every month, 6:00 PM (check Meetup)
Where:
Lower Mathers House, 108 Bathurst Street, Hobart
Who can join:
Everyone — no prior experience needed
Official link
meetup.com/sectalks-hobart

TUSA Clubs & Societies

STUDENT-LED

Find a coding, gaming, culture or sports club run by students. Work on projects, prepare for hackathons, and learn new tech stacks together.

Open in Google Maps ↗
When:
Sign up any time — biggest intake at O-Week and Clubs Day
Where:
Sandy Bay, Inveresk and Newnham campuses
Who can join:
All students — curious beginners welcome
Official link
tusa.org.au — Find a club

Volunteer & Give Back

Volunteering Tasmania

VOLUNTEER

The state hub for volunteering. Search hundreds of roles — community events, food relief, or offering your IT skills to a local non-profit.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Flexible — choose roles that fit your timetable
Where:
Across Hobart and Tasmania
Who can join:
All students — curious beginners welcome
Official link
volunteeringtas.org.au

Rotary Club of Hobart

VOLUNTEERNETWORKING

A welcoming, 100-year-old service club. Use your technical skills to help with web development and IT support for community projects, and meet local professionals.

Open in Google Maps ↗
When:
Thursdays, 1:00 – 2:00 PM
Where:
Hotel Grand Chancellor, 1 Davey Street, Hobart
Who can join:
Students and new members welcome — email the club first
Official link
rotaryhobart.org
